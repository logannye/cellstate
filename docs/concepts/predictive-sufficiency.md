# Predictive sufficiency

The desired representation is the smallest one that preserves distributions of future observations
and functional behavior under the query's interventions, environments, and horizons. There is no
universal minimal state.

The operational adequacy test trains or evaluates two future predictors:

1. state alone -> future;
2. state plus raw history -> future.

If the second materially improves held-out prediction, the state omitted historically relevant
information. Investigate exposure duration, divisions, parent phenotype, accumulated stress, prior
signaling pulses, chromatin, and repeated-treatment history rather than treating the gap as a mere
benchmark score.
